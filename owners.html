What we will not tell you

We report sold-out nights and confirmed bookings separately, and never merge them into one number. A sold-out night at a fractional property may be owner use rather than market demand. A confirmed booking is a unit we watched go from available to gone. Both are useful; only one is evidence of a paying guest, and you will always be told which you are looking at.

The same discipline applies to the roadmap. Booking probability and realised rates need a full year of observed nights, and collection began in August 2026. We are not going to describe them as available before they are.
